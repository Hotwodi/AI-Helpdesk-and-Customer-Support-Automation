AI Helpdesk & Customer Support Automation

AI-powered ticketing, auto-responses, SLA monitoring, knowledge base, and support analytics — all in one Odoo module.

Overview

The AI Helpdesk & Customer Support Automation module brings intelligent automation to your customer support workflow in Odoo 18. From AI-driven sentiment analysis and priority prediction on incoming tickets to automated response templates and proactive SLA breach monitoring, this module helps your team resolve issues faster and keep customers happier.

Key Features

AI Helpdesk Tickets

Tickets with AI sentiment analysis, category prediction, and priority prediction. Full lifecycle from new to closed with response-time tracking.

AI Auto-Responses

Pre-built response templates triggered by keywords, with AI confidence scoring, auto-send toggle, usage counts, and success-rate tracking.

SLA Monitoring

Track first-response and resolution SLAs per ticket. AI risk scoring flags at-risk and breached SLAs before they impact customers.

Knowledge Base

Curated articles with AI relevance scoring, view and helpful counts, tags, and categories to empower agents and self-service.

Support Analytics

Period-based analytics covering total tickets, average response/resolution times, satisfaction, AI automation rate, top category, and escalation rate.
